Battlements

Primarily created in Adobe Illustrator, this piece is a heavy mix of modern day minimalismm of poster design while also adding in my own design aesthetic of adding in artificial grunge, paper folds, and a slight discoloration to make it look weathered.
This was one of my first attempts at using illustrator in any capacity, and I am very proud of how it came out.
