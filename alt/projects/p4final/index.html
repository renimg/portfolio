Falling Letters

Created for the game Dungeon Defenders as one of the rewards for the Eye of Haste vs Eye of Devastation team contest.
Made using Blender and primitive colors to give it the appearance of a solid gold crown embedded with jewels.
This would attach to any of the winning team's eye pets retroactively as proof of victory.
