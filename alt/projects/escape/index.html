Falling Letters

Created for my final for ART-75, a scene inspired heavily by point & click adventure games and the Windows 95 maze screensaver.

This was rendered out in Blender and has 2 versions:
one inspired directly by the Windows 98 screensaver

and another inspired by more modern rendering styles with lighting applied.

I tried to source accurate early-to-mid 90s stock textures, such as using the 90s era Rubik's Cube logo.
and the stone texture supplied with the windows 95 version of the 3D maze screensaver.
The only exception were the things I made by hand, such as the Tuna Can, computer screen, and key. (Listed below)
