Artist Statement

JordanKimberly Smith is a self taught 3D modeler and texture artist living in San Jose, California.
Lover of cats, mid 2000s Video Games, and the color purple. I've always had a fascination and love for all things Video Games. In my free time I work on HI//VOLTAGE, an RC Car racing game I've been developing, writing, and concepting since 2023. I've also been working on "Dungeon Defenders" as a 3D/2D artist since 2025.
